## Before you start {#before}

<GuideHtml html={"<div class=\"callout warning\"><strong>Use with permission</strong><p>Use the M1 with devices you own or have permission to operate. Don’t point an IR transmitter at anyone’s eyes or sensitive optical equipment.</p></div><p>Infrared requires line of sight. Point the M1 transmitter toward the device’s IR receiver.</p>"} />

## Universal Remotes {#universal}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Universal Remotes uses the installed infrared database for common devices.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Infrared → Universal Remotes</div><div class=\"function-block\"><h3>TV remotes</h3><p>TV Remotes gives you common controls for power, sound, and channels.</p>Select each displayed Power, Mute, Volume, or Channel command, aim at the television, and press Center. Keep the M1 aimed at the TV while it tries compatible codes. Press Back to stop.</div><div class=\"function-block\"><h3>Audio player remotes</h3><p>Audio Player Remotes gives you common playback and volume controls.</p>Choose the displayed Power, Play/Pause, Previous/Next, Volume, or Mute command, aim at the player, and press Center.</div><div class=\"function-block\"><h3>Projector remotes</h3><p>Projector Remotes gives you common power and volume controls.</p>Choose the displayed Power, Volume, or Mute command, aim at the projector receiver, and press Center.</div><div class=\"function-block\"><h3>AC remotes</h3><p>AC Remotes gives you common controls for power, temperature, and operating mode.</p>Choose the displayed Power, temperature, heat/cool, or dehumidifier command. Aim at the indoor unit until transmission ends, then verify the unit display.</div><div class=\"callout note\"><strong>No response</strong><p>If the device still doesn’t respond after the M1 tries every saved code, use Learn with the original remote.</p></div>"} />

## Learn {#learn}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Learn receives one command from an original infrared remote and displays the decoded values.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Infrared → Learn</div><ol><li>Aim the original remote at the M1 receiver from a short distance.</li><li>Press one remote button briefly.</li><li>Keep alignment until protocol, address, and command appear.</li><li>If nothing appears, move closer and try a fresh battery.</li><li>Press Back to cancel or to exit after the M1 captures the signal.</li></ol>"} />

## Replay {#replay}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Replay transmits a learned or saved infrared command to the selected device.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Infrared → Replay</div><ol><li>After learning a command, open Replay or choose an infrared file you’ve already saved.</li><li>Review protocol, address, and command.</li><li>Point the M1 at the device you want to operate.</li><li>Press Center to transmit.</li><li>Wait for the signal to finish sending, then check whether the device responds.</li><li>Press Back to stop or return.</li></ol><div class=\"callout note\"><strong>Clear names</strong><p>Use action names such as TV_Power or Projector_Mute when organizing commands.</p></div>"} />

## Remap Tx pin {#remap}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Remap Tx pin selects the built-in infrared transmitter or a supported external output.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Infrared → Remap Tx pin</div><ol><li>Power off and disconnect external hardware.</li><li>Connect the ground, power, and IR output lines exactly as shown.</li><li>Use Left and Right to select the displayed output.</li><li>Press Back after confirming it.</li><li>Test at a safe distance.</li></ol><div class=\"callout warning\"><strong>External driver</strong><p>Do not drive a high-current IR LED directly from an unverified GPIO. Use the documented driver and voltage domain.</p></div>"} />

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

<GuideHtml html={"<div class=\"table-wrap\"><table><thead><tr><th>Problem</th><th>What to do</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Universal command fails</td><td>Let the M1 try all available codes. If none work, use Learn.</td></tr><tr><td>Learn finds nothing</td><td>Move closer, replace battery, and aim directly.</td></tr><tr><td>Fields change</td><td>Use a brief consistent press and avoid strong ambient IR.</td></tr><tr><td>Replay unreliable</td><td>Move closer and check that the transmitter is working.</td></tr><tr><td>Wrong device reacts</td><td>Stop and move other devices out of range before trying again.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>"} />
