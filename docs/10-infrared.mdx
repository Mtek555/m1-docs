## Infrared menu {#infrared-menu}

| Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Universal Remotes | Tries database codes for TVs, audio players, projectors, and air conditioners. |
| Learn | Captures a button from an existing remote. |
| Replay | Opens and transmits saved remote data. |

## Learn a remote button {#learn-command}

1. Open **Infrared > Learn**.
2. Point the remote at the M1 receiver from approximately **5 to 50 cm** away.
3. Press and hold the remote button for about one second.
4. When recognized, review **Protocol**, **Address**, and **Command** on screen.

| Documented learn protocol | Common examples |
| --- | --- |
| NEC | LG, Onkyo, Apple, and others |
| SIRCS | Sony |
| Samsung | Samsung televisions and related devices |
| Kaseikyo | Panasonic, JVC, and related devices |

:::note[Learn versus transmit]
The M1 may transmit additional variants through saved or database files. A protocol being available for transmission does not mean the **Learn** screen can decode it.
:::

## Replay and Universal Remotes {#replay-universal}

- **Replay:** choose a saved remote and transmit its stored command.
- **Universal Remotes:** choose a device category and try database commands until the authorized target responds.

:::warning[Database required]
Universal Remotes requires the official infrared database files on the microSD card.
:::

## Troubleshooting {#ir-troubleshooting}

| Problem | Likely cause | What to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal not recognized | Unsupported learn protocol | Try Universal Remotes or another supported remote. |
| Inconsistent reads | Sunlight or flickering light | Move to a shaded location and aim directly at the receiver. |
| Short transmit range | Low battery or blocked line of sight | Charge the M1 and point the top edge toward the target. |
| Remote is too bright / overdrives | Remote is too close | Increase the learning distance by several centimeters. |

:::warning[Responsible use]
Only control devices you own or are authorized to operate.
:::
