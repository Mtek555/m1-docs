## Electrical safety first {#electrical-safety}

:::danger[5 V tolerance has limits]
External-header signals are **5 V-tolerant only in digital I/O mode and only while the M1 is powered**. Do not drive a pin when the M1 is off. Analog-capable `PA9`, `PA10`, `PC1`, `PC2`, and `PC3` are limited to **VDDA + 0.3 V (about 3.6 V maximum)** when used in analog mode.
:::

- When the GPIO menu opens, controllable pins start as outputs at **Low**.
- Leaving the GPIO menu returns the pins to a safe state and switches off external rails.
- The 3.3 V and 5 V rails cannot be enabled at the same time.
- Use **3.3 V logic** whenever the connected peripheral supports it; 5 V tolerance is a protection limit, not the default signaling voltage.

## GPIO menu {#gpio-menu}

| Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| GPIO Control | Selects a documented pin and toggles its output Low or High. |
| 3.3V power | Enables the 3.3 V rail and disables the 5 V rail. |
| 5V power | Enables the 5 V rail and disables the 3.3 V rail. |
| USB-UART bridge | Bridges USB CDC to external UART1 on PA9 TX / PA10 RX at 3.3 V, or to the internal ESP32 UART. |

## Control a pin {#control-pin}

1. Open **GPIO > GPIO Control**.
2. Use **Left / Right** to select a controllable signal.
3. Press **Center** to toggle the output between Low and High.
4. Press **Back** when finished so the M1 can return pins and rails to their exit state.

The documented control screen includes `PE2`, `PE4`, `PE5`, `PE6`, `PD12`, `PD13`, `PA14`, `PA13`, `PC2`, `PC3`, `PD0`, and `PD1`. `PA9` and `PA10` are reserved for the functional USB-UART bridge.

## GPIO current limits {#gpio-current}

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Recommended design current | Approximately 8 mA or less per pin for reliable logic-level margins |
| Absolute maximum per pin | 20 mA sourced or sunk; do not design normal loads to this ceiling |
| Combined I/O source current | 140 mA maximum across all I/O pins |
| Combined I/O sink current | 140 mA maximum across all I/O pins |

:::danger[Absolute maximum is not an operating target]
The **20 mA per-pin** and **140 mA aggregate** figures are STM32H573 silicon absolute maximum ratings. Exceeding them can cause incorrect operation or permanent damage. Use external drivers for higher-current loads.
:::

## Use the USB-UART bridge {#usb-uart-bridge}

1. Open **GPIO > USB-UART bridge**.
2. Use **Left / Right** to select **UART: TX (PA9), RX (PA10), 3.3 V** for the external header or **UART: ESP32** for the internal ESP32 connection.
3. Press **Center** to start the selected bridge.
4. Open the M1 USB CDC serial port on the connected computer. The default is **115200 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit**; supported host line-coding changes are applied to the UART.
5. Press **Back** to stop the bridge and return to the GPIO menu.

:::note[Bridge targets]
Choose the external UART1 header or the internal ESP32 connection.
:::

## Power-rail limits {#power-rails}

- Do not exceed **0.5 A** on the **3.3 V EXT** output.
- Do not exceed **0.5 A** on the **5 V EXT** output.
- Do not power motors, solenoids, relays, heaters, or other high-current loads directly from the M1.
- Do not feed external power back into an enabled M1 rail.
- Use a common ground between the M1 and the external circuit.
- Treat analog-capable `PA9`, `PA10`, `PC1`, `PC2`, and `PC3` as approximately **3.6 V maximum** when used in analog mode.
