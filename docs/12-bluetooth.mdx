<p className="guideLede">Discover BLE devices, inspect and save advertising data, measure signal strength, and browse GATT services.</p>

## Before you start {#before}

<GuideHtml html={"<div class=\"callout warning\"><strong>Before you connect</strong><p>BLE addresses, names, manufacturer data, services, and values can identify people or equipment. Connect or write only with authorization.</p></div><p>Leave Wi-Fi before opening Bluetooth.</p>"} />

## Scan Devices {#scan}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Scan Devices discovers nearby Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements and displays their available identity and advertising data.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Bluetooth → Scan Devices</div><ol><li>Wait while the M1 finds nearby devices.</li><li>Browse by name or address; unnamed entries are numbered for the session.</li><li>Press Center on Details.</li><li>Use Left and Right through identity, RSSI, flags, UUIDs, manufacturer data, and parsed fields.</li><li>Press Center on Save to name a live record under ble/.</li><li>Press Back to the device list, then Back to leave.</li></ol><div class=\"callout note\"><strong>Advertisement snapshot</strong><p>A saved record is not a pairing record and does not prove the device is connectable.</p></div>"} />

## Signal Meter {#meter}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Signal Meter tracks the selected device’s received signal strength to help with relative proximity checks.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Bluetooth → Signal Meter</div><ol><li>Select the target.</li><li>Wait while the M1 measures the signal.</li><li>Read averaged RSSI and signal category.</li><li>Move slowly with consistent orientation.</li><li>On Signal lost, move closer or confirm advertising continues.</li><li>Press Back to stop.</li></ol><p>RSSI shows relative signal strength, not exact distance. Walls, people, orientation, interference, interval, and transmit power affect it.</p>"} />

## GATT Explorer {#gatt}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>GATT Explorer connects to a BLE device and browses its services, characteristics, and supported actions.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Bluetooth → GATT Explorer</div><ol><li>Select a device and press Center on Connect.</li><li>Wait for connection and service discovery.</li><li>Select a service, then a characteristic.</li><li>Choose an action supported by the characteristic.</li><li>Press Back one level at a time to return or disconnect.</li></ol><div class=\"table-wrap\"><table><thead><tr><th>Action</th><th>Instructions</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Details</td><td>Review UUID, handle, properties, and descriptors.</td></tr><tr><td>Read</td><td>Request current length and hexadecimal bytes.</td></tr><tr><td>Write</td><td>Enter even-length hex up to 64 bytes and send with response.</td></tr><tr><td>Write No Rsp</td><td>Send even-length hex without application confirmation.</td></tr><tr><td>Notify</td><td>Subscribe via CCCD and watch notification values; Back stops.</td></tr><tr><td>Indicate</td><td>Subscribe to acknowledged indications when supported; Back stops.</td></tr></tbody></table></div><div class=\"callout warning\"><strong>Writes change devices</strong><p>Do not write unless the exact characteristic, value, and expected effect are documented.</p></div>"} />

## Saved {#saved}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Saved opens previously stored BLE advertisement snapshots without making a live connection.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Bluetooth → Saved</div><ol><li>Select a .ble file under ble/.</li><li>Press Center to open it.</li><li>Use Left and Right through stored details.</li><li>Compare with a new scan when current state matters.</li><li>Press Back to close.</li></ol><p>Opening a saved record does not connect to the device or provide live GATT controls.</p>"} />

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

<GuideHtml html={"<div class=\"table-wrap\"><table><thead><tr><th>Problem</th><th>What to do</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>No devices</td><td>Wait, move closer, and confirm advertising.</td></tr><tr><td>Radio busy</td><td>Leave Wi-Fi.</td></tr><tr><td>Connect failed</td><td>Confirm connectable, nearby, and not connected elsewhere.</td></tr><tr><td>Read failed</td><td>Security or pairing may be required.</td></tr><tr><td>Not subscribable</td><td>No usable CCCD or Notify/Indicate property.</td></tr><tr><td>Write rejected</td><td>Verify properties, security, byte format, and authorization.</td></tr><tr><td>Saved empty</td><td>Check .ble files under ble/ and card mount.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>"} />
