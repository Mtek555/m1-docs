## Before you start {#before}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Wi-Fi includes tools for viewing nearby networks, checking channel activity, capturing diagnostic data, and running focused network tests.</p><p>Exit Bluetooth before opening Wi-Fi. Insert a writable card for saved networks, handshakes, beacon lists, or PCAPNG captures.</p>"} />

## Networks {#networks}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Networks scans nearby 2.4 GHz access points and lets you review, save, and inspect their associated devices.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Wi-Fi → Networks</div><ol><li>Wait for the AP scan or cached list.</li><li>Use Left and Right to move through the network results; use Rescan for a fresh result.</li><li>Press Center on Actions.</li><li>Choose Scan for Devices to look for associated stations.</li><li>Choose Save Network to add or update SSID, BSSID, channel, RSSI, and security.</li><li>Choose Network Details for the full AP summary.</li><li>Choose Saved Networks to inspect the SD list.</li><li>In Saved Networks, choose Actions → Delete and confirm the intended entry.</li></ol><div class=\"callout note\"><strong>Saved network</strong><p>The saved entry includes the network name, address, channel, signal strength, and security type. It does not include the password.</p></div>"} />

## Deauth Client {#deauth}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Deauth Client performs a short disconnect test against a selected device.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Wi-Fi → Deauth Client</div><ol><li>Choose Use Selected when the correct AP is already selected, or Scan Networks.</li><li>Open the selected AP’s Actions.</li><li>Choose Target Devices, or Details to verify the AP first.</li><li>Select the exact station and review Device Details.</li><li>Verify network and device on the confirmation screen.</li><li>Choose Start and keep the test brief.</li><li>Press Center or Back to stop.</li><li>Use Change Device, Change Network, or Rescan before another test.</li></ol><div class=\"callout note\"><strong>Short connection test</strong><p>Deauth Client briefly disconnects the selected device from its network. Use it for authorized testing and stop when you have the result you need.</p></div>"} />

## MonstaShark {#monstashark}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>MonstaShark records Wi-Fi traffic on a selected channel as a PCAPNG file for later analysis.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Wi-Fi → MonstaShark</div><ol><li>Select an AP; use Rescan for fresh results.</li><li>Verify SSID and fixed channel.</li><li>Use Up or Down for capture duration.</li><li>Press Center on Run.</li><li>Watch packet, byte, drop, and storage counters on both result pages.</li><li>Press Center or Back to stop cleanly.</li><li>Review the saved PCAPNG filename under wifi/.</li><li>Transfer the capture through USB Drive and open it in Wireshark for review.</li></ol><div class=\"callout note\"><strong>Manual mode</strong><p>When no AP is found, Left/Right changes channel and Up/Down changes duration; confirm the channel independently before Run.</p></div>"} />

## Handshake {#handshake}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Handshake captures WPA authentication traffic from a selected network and saves complete or partial results for analysis.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Wi-Fi → Handshake</div><ol><li>Choose Use Selected or Scan Networks.</li><li>Select the AP and verify channel.</li><li>Choose Start Capture.</li><li>Watch stages and packet count.</li><li>Press Center or Back to stop when needed.</li><li>For a complete result, choose Save Capture and name it.</li><li>For an incomplete result, deliberately choose Save Capture, Retry, or Back.</li><li>At Capture Stopped, choose Save Capture or Discard.</li></ol><p>Saved captures are stored under <code>wifi/</code> and can be transferred through USB Drive.</p>"} />

## Beacon {#beacon}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Beacon transmits test network names over 2.4 GHz from an SD list or a name entered on the keypad.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Wi-Fi → Beacon</div><div class=\"table-wrap\"><table><thead><tr><th>Source</th><th>Instructions</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>SD List</td><td>Put one SSID per line in wifi/ap_lists.txt; up to 16 non-empty entries, 32 bytes each.</td></tr><tr><td>Keypad SSID</td><td>Enter one SSID and verify it before transmission.</td></tr></tbody></table></div><ol><li>Select the source.</li><li>Review the running names and count.</li><li>Use it in a controlled test environment and stop when finished.</li><li>Press Center or Back to stop.</li><li>Wait for Stopping before leaving.</li></ol><div class=\"callout note\"><strong>Fallback</strong><p>If the SD list is empty, the M1 may use the currently selected network name. Check the running screen.</p></div>"} />

## Channel Survey {#survey}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Channel Survey summarizes nearby 2.4 GHz activity by channel using access-point count and strongest signal.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Wi-Fi → Channel Survey</div><ol><li>Wait for the one-shot 2.4 GHz scan.</li><li>Read total detected APs.</li><li>Use Left and Right to move through channels 1–14.</li><li>Compare AP count and strongest RSSI.</li><li>Press Center to rescan.</li><li>Press Back to exit.</li></ol><p>Fewer APs does not automatically mean a better channel; consider signal strength, width, interference, and regulatory domain.</p>"} />

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

<GuideHtml html={"<div class=\"table-wrap\"><table><thead><tr><th>Problem</th><th>What to do</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>ESP32 not ready</td><td>Leave, try bridge reset, and reopen Wi-Fi.</td></tr><tr><td>Radio busy</td><td>Leave Bluetooth completely.</td></tr><tr><td>No APs</td><td>Move closer, rescan, and confirm supported 2.4 GHz channels.</td></tr><tr><td>No devices</td><td>Rescan; the station may be idle, randomized, or unassociated.</td></tr><tr><td>Capture link failed</td><td>Retry once after leaving BLE; then verify ESP32 firmware pairing.</td></tr><tr><td>Save failed</td><td>Check mount, free space, and USB Drive ownership.</td></tr><tr><td>Handshake incomplete</td><td>Verify channel and activity; stay inside the approved window.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>"} />
