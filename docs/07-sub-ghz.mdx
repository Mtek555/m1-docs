## Scan {#scan}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Scan listens for nearby Sub-GHz activity and attempts to identify supported signal protocols and their decoded values.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Sub-GHz → Scan</div><ol><li>Confirm the displayed frequency and modulation.</li><li>Press Center to listen.</li><li>Activate the authorized remote briefly near the M1.</li><li>Watch the signal strength and decoded result.</li><li>Use Left or Right where the screen offers another frequency or result.</li><li>Press Center on Stop, or Back, to end reception.</li></ol><div class=\"table-wrap\"><table><thead><tr><th>Field</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Frequency</td><td>Tuned receive frequency.</td></tr><tr><td>Modulation</td><td>OOK, ASK, or FSK receive mode.</td></tr><tr><td>Protocol</td><td>Decoder that recognized the signal.</td></tr><tr><td>Bits</td><td>Decoded payload length.</td></tr><tr><td>Key / Payload</td><td>Captured value; treat it as credential data.</td></tr><tr><td>RSSI</td><td>Received strength; less-negative values are stronger.</td></tr></tbody></table></div><div class=\"callout note\"><strong>If the signal is not identified</strong><p>Move closer to the transmitter and try reading it again. If the M1 still cannot identify the signal, use Read RAW to record a short sample instead. Some signal types cannot be decoded by the M1.</p></div>"} />

## Record {#record}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Record saves the timing of a Sub-GHz signal as a RAW capture when decoded protocol data is not required or available.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Sub-GHz → Record</div><ol><li>Use Left and Right on Ready to select a fixed frequency; modulation changes automatically for the range.</li><li>Press Center on Start.</li><li>Operate the authorized transmitter briefly.</li><li>Press Center or Back to stop and finalize the temporary capture.</li><li>Press Center to replay once. After completion, hold Center to repeat only while held.</li><li>Press Down on Save to name and keep the recording.</li><li>Press Left or Back from Complete to discard and return to Ready.</li></ol><div class=\"callout warning\"><strong>Overflow</strong><p>If buffer or sample overflow appears, discard the capture, shorten the recording, reduce unrelated RF activity, and retry.</p></div>"} />

## Replay {#replay}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Replay transmits a previously saved Sub-GHz capture using its stored frequency and modulation settings.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Sub-GHz → Replay</div><ol><li>Select a compatible saved file.</li><li>Review its frequency and modulation.</li><li>Point the M1 or connected transmitter toward the receiver.</li><li>Press Center to transmit once. Hold it to repeat only when you have permission to do so.</li><li>Release Center to stop repeating and press Back to return.</li></ol><div class=\"callout warning\"><strong>Rolling codes</strong><p>Repeated replay can desynchronize an authorized rolling-code remote without operating the receiver.</p></div>"} />

## Frequency Reader {#frequency-reader}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Frequency Reader helps identify the frequency of a strong nearby Sub-GHz transmission.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Sub-GHz → Frequency Reader</div><ol><li>Keep the source close to the M1.</li><li>Press the authorized transmitter briefly.</li><li>Wait for the detected frequency to stabilize.</li><li>Repeat from a slightly different position if it jumps or disappears.</li><li>Record the stable result and press Back.</li></ol><p>This identifies a strong carrier, not protocol compatibility or permission to transmit.</p>"} />

## Remap Tx pin {#remap}

<GuideHtml html={"<p>Remap Tx pin selects whether Sub-GHz transmissions use the built-in output or a supported external transmitter connection.</p><div class=\"menu-path\"><strong>Menu path</strong>Main Menu → Sub-GHz → Remap Tx pin</div><ol><li>Disconnect external hardware before wiring.</li><li>Use Left and Right to select the displayed output.</li><li>Use the built-in path for normal operation.</li><li>Select an external path only with the matching M1 pinout and module instructions.</li><li>Press Back after confirming the selection.</li></ol><div class=\"callout warning\"><strong>External transmitter</strong><p>Confirm ground, voltage, connector orientation, and current before connecting a module.</p></div>"} />

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

<GuideHtml html={"<div class=\"table-wrap\"><table><thead><tr><th>Problem</th><th>What to do</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>No signal</td><td>Move closer, verify the source battery, and try the expected frequency.</td></tr><tr><td>No decode</td><td>Try the correct modulation or make a short RAW recording.</td></tr><tr><td>Record will not start</td><td>Check mount state, free space, and write access.</td></tr><tr><td>Replay fails</td><td>Verify file, frequency, output selection, compatibility, and regional permission.</td></tr><tr><td>Tuning failed</td><td>Return and choose another supported frequency.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>"} />
