## Sub-GHz menu {#subghz-menu}

:::warning[Before you start]
A microSD card is required for **Record** and **Replay**. Capture or transmit only signals from equipment you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
:::

| Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Record | Captures a signal and saves it to the microSD card. |
| Replay | Transmits a previously saved `.sgh` recording. |
| Frequency Reader | Sweeps for an active signal and displays frequency and RSSI. |

## Record a signal {#record-signal}

1. Open **Sub-GHz > Record**.
2. Use **Left / Right** to choose the frequency.
3. Press **Center** to begin recording.
4. Activate the authorized remote or transmitter.
5. Press **Center** or **Back** to stop.
6. Use the on-screen action to save, load for replay, or discard the capture.

Saved captures use the `.sgh` extension and are stored in `/SUBGHZ`.

## Replay a saved signal {#replay-signal}

1. Open **Sub-GHz > Replay**.
2. Select a saved `.sgh` file.
3. Press **Center** to transmit the recording.

:::note[Rolling-code systems]
Raw replay normally will not operate systems using rolling codes, challenge-response authentication, or encryption. That is expected behavior.
:::

## Use Frequency Reader {#frequency-reader}

- Sweeps the configured bands automatically.
- Displays detected frequency and RSSI signal strength.
- Keeps recent detections visible and sounds a beep when a signal is found.
- Locates an active frequency; it does not identify or decode the protocol.

Documented presets: `300.000`, `310.000`, `315.000`, `345.000`, `372.000`, `390.000`, `433.000`, `433.920`, and `915.000 MHz`.

## Antenna hardware {#subghz-antenna}

| Item | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Helical multiband monopole |
| Gain | −7 dBi |
| Dimensions | 38.1 × 8.9 mm |
| Covered bands | 315 / 433 / 868 / 915 MHz |

## Troubleshooting {#subghz-troubleshooting}

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Recording will not start | Insert and mount a readable microSD card. |
| Replay list is empty | Confirm `.sgh` files are in `/SUBGHZ`. |
| Frequency Reader finds nothing | Move the transmitter closer and trigger it repeatedly while scanning. |
| Replay has no effect | Confirm frequency, distance, and target. The source may use a rolling or encrypted protocol. |
