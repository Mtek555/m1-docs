## Charge the M1 {#charge-the-m1}

1. Connect the USB-C cable to the M1.
2. Connect the other end to a standard **5 V USB power source**.
3. Leave the device connected until charging is complete. The M1 may be used while it charges.

| Indicator | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Red LED on | The battery is charging. |
| Red LED off | Charging may be complete, disconnected, or not started. Check **Battery Info** when uncertain. |

## Check battery status {#battery-info}

Open **Settings > Power > Battery Info**. The screen reports battery level, temperature, voltage, health, and charging status.

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not charging | No active charging cycle. |
| Pre-charge | The battery is being recovered at low current. |
| Fast Charge | Normal charging is in progress. |
| Complete | The charging cycle has finished. |

:::danger[Battery safety]
Disconnect the M1 if the enclosure becomes unusually hot, damaged, swollen, or wet. Do not continue charging a visibly damaged device.
:::

## Troubleshooting {#charging-troubleshooting}

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Red LED never turns on | Try another cable and a known-good 5 V source. Check the USB-C port for debris. |
| Battery level does not increase | Restart the M1 and review **Battery Info**. Contact support if voltage or temperature readings are abnormal. |
| Device shuts down quickly | Charge fully, update to the current official firmware, and compare the **Level** and **Health** readings. |
